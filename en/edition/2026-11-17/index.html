Ukraine: Moscow says it intercepted a record 1,336 drones

Russia's Defence Ministry says it intercepted 1,336 Ukrainian drones overnight from 15 to 16 November, over fifteen regions, occupied Crimea and two seas. According to Moscow's mayor, about 600 drones were heading for the capital's region and 191 were intercepted on their approach. Kyiv has not commented and the figures could not be independently verified.

Why it matters If the figures are accurate, this is the largest Ukrainian drone attack on Russia since the war began.
