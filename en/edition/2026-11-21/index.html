2027 presidential race: Dominique de Villepin officially a candidate

The former prime minister made his candidacy for the 2027 presidential election official on Friday, November 20, at a press conference in Paris. He presents himself as an alternative to the far-right Rassemblement national, the hard-left La France insoumise and President Macron's camp. Polling at around 3% of voting intentions, he still has to gather the required sponsorships from elected officials.

Why it matters His entry adds one more candidate to an already fragmented political landscape, less than a year and a half before the vote.

Public sector: new strike call for December 1

The public-sector union coalition is calling for a new day of strikes and protests on Tuesday, December 1, the fourth since the start of the autumn. The unions are demanding that the "point d'indice", the base used to calculate civil servants' pay, be unfrozen. They are also inviting private-sector workers to march alongside public-sector staff.

Why it matters The mobilisation over civil servants' pay is becoming a long-running dispute and weighs on the government's budget talks.

European Commission confirms an AI-assisted cyberattack

The European Commission confirmed on Thursday, November 19 that artificial intelligence tools were used in a cyberattack detected on October 22. Internal staff systems, including payroll and medical-expense reimbursement, were disrupted. The Commission did not say who was behind the attack.

Why it matters It is the first time the European Union has publicly acknowledged a cyberattack carried out with the help of AI, a sign of how vulnerable institutions are.
