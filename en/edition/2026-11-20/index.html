In Shenzhen, Trump, Xi and Putin sign a "peace declaration"

Meeting in China on Thursday, November 19 for an unprecedented three-way summit, the American, Chinese and Russian leaders issued a short declaration stating that a nuclear war cannot be won. The text provides for no talks on a nuclear arms reduction treaty, and no agreement was reached on Ukraine or Iran. Vladimir Putin said Ukraine and Europe were not ready to make compromises.

Why it matters The meeting sets the tone between the three nuclear powers, but for now changes nothing about the war in Ukraine, on Europe's doorstep.
