Nexperia swamped as AI soaks up chipmaking capacity

Dutch semiconductor maker Nexperia is "totally oversubscribed", its interim CEO told Reuters on Tuesday at the Electronica trade fair in Munich. Suppliers are shifting capacity towards AI data centres, at the expense of the mature chips used in cars and industrial equipment.

Why it matters These bottlenecks threaten Europe's car industry, a major buyer of such components.
