Cost of living: 404 arrests on the sidelines of Saturday's protests

The Interior Ministry announced on Sunday that 404 people were arrested in France on the sidelines of the protests on Saturday 14 November, including 126 in Paris. Ten police officers were slightly injured. In Paris, 108 people were taken into custody, 38 of them minors, according to the police prefecture.

Why it matters It was the fifth consecutive Saturday of protests: the arrest figures show tensions persist despite falling turnout.
