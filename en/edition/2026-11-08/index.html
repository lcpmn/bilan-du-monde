Blight-resistant rice thanks to an AI-designed tool

University of Missouri researchers made rice plants resistant to bacterial blight, a disease that threatens harvests in Asia and Africa, using OpenCRISPR-1, an AI-designed, freely usable gene-editing tool. Published in the journal aBIOTECH, the work is at the lab stage: commercial varieties are several years away.

SourceUniversité du Missouri ↗“Mizzou researchers create rice plants that fight off devastating disease”showme.missouri.edu
